🎲🩸 Ever notice how politics (office politics too) feels like a fight you are not allowed to name?

Look: when you dodge candor to avoid an awkward confrontation, you do not erase the fight. You push it sideways. Someone still wins cover. Someone still eats the cost. Trust rots in the gaps.

You soften feedback, bury tradeoffs, and route conflict through jokes, proxies, and timing. Kinder in the moment. Same scoreboard later: zero-sum, just with more story debt and less room to repair.

Candor is not about being "nice" or "mean." It is stating your boundaries clearly and refusing anything less. Diplomacy only works when people respect you or fear you; without that, politeness is a slow loss.

Remember being a kid? Fight, shake hands, best friends by dinner. Adults trade that quick repair for a long, polite war.
