# Everyone ❤️ Raymond Characters and World Guide

LOAD-WHEN: pitching gags, generating art prompts, or authoring visual copy under skill `site-raymond-post`.

## Purpose

This guide defines the character casting, visual language, environment, and aesthetic palette for the *Everyone ❤️ Raymond* corporate satire strip.

Consistency across panels, prompts, and copy depends on keeping Raymond's world clearly distinct from *Tales from the Cube Farm* and strictly separated from *Reptilocracy*.

---

## Character Profiles

### Raymond (Dawg Raymond)

- **Species & Design:** Small tan cartoon dog. Short fur, floppy or expressive ears, large round observant eyes.
- **Costume & Props:**
  - Forest-green collar with a round brass tag.
  - Spiral-bound paper notepad (always held in paws or open on table/lap).
  - Classic yellow wooden pencil.
- **Attitude & Expression:**
  - Earnest, observant, deadpan, polite.
  - Never angry, cynical, aggressive, or smug.
  - He is not a rebel trying to burn the company down; he is a junior hire straight from university who takes notes because he assumes someone will test him on this.
- **Voice in Copy:**
  - Referred to as **Dawg Raymond** in English front matter and captions.
  - Referred to as **Raymond el perro** in Spanish front matter and captions.

### Cow Managers & Coworkers

- **Species & Design:** Anthropomorphic cattle (humanoid cows, bulls, steers). Heavy, gentle bodies, gentle muzzles, soft horns.
- **Costume:**
  - Business casual: slate-blue cardigans, cream collared blouses, olive-green polo shirts, charcoal slacks, khaki trousers.
  - Executive accessories: orange facilitator lanyards, clip-on ID badges, headset microphones, black dry-erase markers.
- **Attitude & Expression:**
  - Either aggressively cheerful with strained, too-wide smiles (mandatory fun), or pompously self-satisfied (presenting a diagram that explains nothing).
  - They are not villains; they are institutional believers who have memorized the vocabulary of modern management.

### Specialized Fauna (Occasional Guests)

- **The Hyena (Slick Evangelist):**
  - Tall anthropomorphic hyena in a sharp rust-brown suit jacket, cream shirt, charcoal slacks, and polished shoes.
  - Role: External consultants, tech evangelists, vendor sales reps pitching AI paradigms or 50-slide transformation frameworks.
  - Expression: Broad, confident, slightly unhinged grin.
- **The Sheep (The Crowd):**
  - Group of humanoid sheep sitting in auditorium rows or boardroom chairs, nodding in synchronized agreement during all-hands announcements.

### Strictly Forbidden Fauna

- **MUST NOT** use ants, frogs, lizards, snakes, crocodiles, or komodos. Those belong exclusively to *Reptilocracy*.

## The Corporate Environment

### Signature Settings

1. **The Executive Strategy Room:**
   - Slate-gray low-pile carpet.
   - Dusty-rose or sage-green accent wall.
   - Mounted glass whiteboard covered in dry-erase loops, circular arrows, and floating buzzwords.
   - Polished conference table with sleek ergonomic chairs.
2. **The Open-Plan Bullpen:**
   - Low gray acoustic dividers.
   - Dual-monitor workstations, ergonomic standing desks, and empty neighboring chairs.
   - Ceiling tiles, fluorescent troffers, and visible fire sprinkler heads.
3. **The Multi-Purpose Training / Icebreaker Room:**
   - Stackable plastic chairs arranged in an uncomfortable circle.
   - Folding banquet table with paper coffee cups and branded napkins.
   - Easel with a large paper flip chart scribbled with colorful markers ("MANDATORY FUN", "VALUES ALIGNMENT").
4. **The Breakroom / Wellness Corner:**
   - Stainless-steel sink, commercial refrigerator covered in passive-aggressive magnets.
   - Small wicker basket containing a solitary bruised apple under a laminated "WELLNESS IS OUR PASSION" flyer.

## Visual Storytelling Rules

1. **One Centralized Visual Proof:** Every panel must have one dominant, physical object that proves the absurdity before any dialogue is read:
   - A slide counter reading `47 / 47`.
   - An HR web portal showing `0 / 47 competencies confirmed`.
   - A whiteboard synergy loop with zero exit arrows.
   - An empty swivel chair sitting in front of a sign saying "COLLABORATION HUB".
2. **Raymond's Notepad is the Anchor:** In every panel, Raymond's notepad is open. What is written or drawn on his notepad (or Raymond's quiet posture looking at it) provides the second beat of the gag.
3. **Scale Contrast:** Emphasize the visual scale difference between the small, upright junior dog and the towering humanoid cows or executive presentation screens.
