# Everyone ❤️ Raymond Theme Catalog

LOAD-WHEN: pitching Raymond comic strips or selecting joke premises under skill `site-raymond-post`.

**Author premise:**
Raymond is the junior dog from *Cube-Cows* (*Just smaller*), fresh out of university and stepping into corporate life with an open spiral notepad. He does not rebel, preach, or attempt to fix the culture. He takes literal, forensic notes. The comedy comes from treating corporate buzzwords as adult kindergarten: management announces an abstract virtue, and Raymond's earnest compliance exposes the hollow space underneath.

**Rules:**
- MUST cite an `R-T#` id when pitching gags.
- MUST keep the three-beat engine intact: Corporate Mandate, Literal Void, Forensic Note.
- NEVER let Raymond express open cynicism, rage, or preachiness; his superpower is deadpan, innocent observation.
- NEVER use Reptilocracy species (ants, frogs, lizards); Raymond lives in the Cube-Cows corporate animal universe (cows, bulls, sheep, hyenas).

## Catalog of Themes

### R-T1: Physical Presence Theater (Return to Office)
- **Corporate Mandate:** "True collaboration and cultural synergy require physical presence in the office."
- **Literal Void:** The office is empty, teammates are on Zoom with headsets across cubes, or the employee sits isolated in a cubicle.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond complies by holding a formal, high-stakes meeting with an empty swivel chair across from him.
- **Shipped Reference:** R1 (*Collaborating with myself*)

### R-T2: Mandatory Fun & Synthetic Morale
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Spontaneous team joy, bonding icebreakers, and high energy are essential core values."
- **Literal Void:** A sterile room, stiff fake smiles, hostage-video body language, and plastic name badges.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond sits calmly in the circle taking clinical field notes on the exact enforcement mechanisms of "mandatory fun."
- **Shipped Reference:** R11 (*Mandatory enthusiasm*)

### R-T3: The Infinite Synergy Map
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Our holistic, matrixed framework harmonizes cross-functional value streams."
- **Literal Void:** A massive whiteboard diagram with dozens of boxes, loops, and arrows that never lead to an actual decision box.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond follows the arrows with his finger and notepad, noting that every arrow loops indefinitely except the one labeled "decision," which does not exist.
- **Shipped Reference:** R12 (*The synergy map*)

### R-T4: Forced Calibration & Bell-Curve Theater
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Our performance calibration is completely fair, objective, and totally aligned across all departments."
- **Literal Void:** Predetermined quotas where management agrees in advance that almost no one can exceed expectations, regardless of actual output.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond watches the projector screen where every employee is rated "Exceeds Expectations" except his solitary row marked "Developing," which he faithfully copies down.
- **Shipped Reference:** R13 (*Calibration crisis*)

### R-T5: Competency Checklist Proliferation
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Every knowledge worker must demonstrate forty-seven bespoke behavioral competencies in the HR talent portal."
- **Literal Void:** Actual delivered work has no checkbox in the portal; the entire exercise is a test of buzzword stamina.
- **Satire Engine:** While peers click wildly on unverified competencies, Raymond submits a single line with empirical evidence: "writing things down."
- **Shipped Reference:** R14 (*Competency log*)

### R-T6: Deck Theater & Innovation Evangelism
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Our forward-leaning innovation deck proves our technical mastery of cutting-edge AI."
- **Literal Void:** A 50-slide presentation filled with buzzwords and unread diagrams that no executive has inspected or verified.
- **Satire Engine:** An external presenter or executive hyena boasts on slide 47 that new tooling catches every loose end, prompting Raymond to ask why no one used it to read the presentation.
- **Shipped Reference:** R10 (*Loose ends in the deck*)

### R-T7: Wellness Tokenism
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Employee well-being and psychological safety are prioritized through our curated wellness experience."
- **Literal Void:** Chronic 70-hour weeks and systemic understaffing paired with an insulting, cost-free token.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond investigates the breakroom wellness basket containing a single bruised apple, calculating its nutritional offset against 40 hours of overtime.
- **Shipped Reference:** R9 (*Wellness is one apple*)

### R-T8: Circular Reporting & Reorg Loops
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Our flattened, agile realignment enhances accountability and cross-functional autonomy."
- **Literal Void:** The reporting structure forms a closed feedback loop where nobody has managerial responsibility.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond traces his organizational chart line through multiple dotted arrows until the path terminates directly back at his own name.
- **Shipped Reference:** R6 (*Reporting to yourself*)

### R-T9: Feedback Sandwiches & Coded Criticism
- **Corporate Mandate:** "We foster a culture of radical candor delivered through supportive coaching conversations."
- **Literal Void:** Uncomfortable criticism buried between thick layers of generic, confusing compliments.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond treats his performance appraisal like a culinary diagnosis, dissecting the synthetic breading to locate the microscopic complaint inside.
- **Shipped Reference:** R7 (*Diagnosing sandwich*)

### R-T10: Open Door / Selective Deafness
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Leadership maintains a strict open-door policy so every junior voice is heard."
- **Literal Void:** The physical door remains open while the manager wears noise-canceling headphones or ignores questions.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond logs an exhaustive inventory of unanswered one-on-one requests while standing directly in front of the wide-open office door.
- **Shipped Reference:** R2 (*Door open, ears closed*)

### R-T11: AI Adoption Panic & Dread Mimicry
- **Corporate Mandate:** "We are rapidly adopting generative AI tools to augment our operational excellence."
- **Literal Void:** Nobody knows what the tools do; management simply wants to see staff typing prompts into laptops.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond trains his machine learning prompt to accurately simulate his exact level of junior existential dread.
- **Shipped Reference:** R3 (*Training my replacement*)

### R-T12: Labor Theft as "Team Player" Spirit
- **Corporate Mandate:** "We celebrate collaborative selflessness and going the extra mile for the mission."
- **Literal Void:** Coworkers or managers vanishing at 5:00 PM while claiming credit for work completed over the weekend.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond is awarded "Team Player of the Month" at the exact moment his weekend labor is repackaged as a group victory.
- **Shipped Reference:** R4 (*Team player of the month*)

### R-T13: Meeting About the Pre-Meeting
- **Corporate Mandate:** "We must align stakeholder sentiment prior to the executive alignment session."
- **Literal Void:** Productive work is delayed so three preliminary meetings can rehearse the agenda for the actual meeting.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond fills an entire notebook page tracking calendar invites whose sole stated agenda is planning when to schedule the discussion.

### R-T14: Surveillance Disguised as Empowerment
- **Corporate Mandate:** "Our smart activity analytics platform helps staff balance their cognitive focus time."
- **Literal Void:** Keystroke loggers, mouse-jiggler paranoia, and micro-tracking software monitoring bathroom breaks.
- **Satire Engine:** Raymond times his breathing and notebook scribbles to match the proprietary activity algorithm's preferred cadence.
