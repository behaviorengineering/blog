# Reptilocracy Dark Triad catalog

LOAD-WHEN: pitching Reptilocracy jokes or listing themes under skill `site-reptilocracy-themes`.

Author lock (verbatim): most political leaders are in the dark triad spectrum, and therefore share all the behaviour pathological traits those conditions expose. Satire brings that problem to the light because the rest of people cant see what is in front of their nose. In Reptilocracy panels, **ants** are the public; MUST NOT use **cows** (Cube-Cows is a different hub).

**Rules:**

- MUST cite a `T#` id when pitching

## Narcissism

### T1 Grandiose delusion mistaken for bold vision

- **Pathology:** Megalomania, magical thinking, ego fused with the state
- **Herd misread:** "He is ambitious; he must have a plan"
- **Satire engine:** Physically impossible promise treated as a roadmap

**Rules:** MUST show the impossibility in the frame. NEVER let clerks debate implementation details as if the plan were real.

CORRECT:
```text
Lizard repeals gravity. Crowd takes notes on the ambitious Q3 rollout.
```

PROHIBITED:
```text
Leader is confident and the public likes confidence.
```

### T4 DARVO / victimhood reversal

- **Pathology:** Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender when asked for a receipt
- **Herd misread:** "He is under constant unfair attack"
- **Satire engine:** Predator still holding the stolen thing while demanding an apology

**Rules:** MUST keep the claw in the cookie jar on camera. NEVER make the auditor the actual villain.

CORRECT:
```text
Claw in the public safe. Lizard files a complaint about the hostile tone of the math.
```

### T9 Rules-are-for-little-people entitlement

- **Pathology:** Laws, ethics, and bills exist only for the herd
- **Herd misread:** "Everyone bends the rules a little; it is paperwork"
- **Satire engine:** A conflict-of-interest form treated as an assassination attempt

**Rules:** MUST contrast a tiny compliance chore with a huge theft. NEVER equate staff tardiness with the leader's exemption.

## Machiavellianism

### T3 Manufactured crisis (arsonist as firefighter)

- **Pathology:** Start the fire so you can sell the hose and the emergency stamp
- **Herd misread:** "Thank goodness a strong hand is here in dangerous times"
- **Satire engine:** Gas can still in claw during the prevention ribbon-cutting

**Rules:** MUST leave the cause of the crisis in the same panel as the rescue pose. NEVER hide the matches offstage.

CORRECT:
```text
Leaking gas can. Ribbon on the Ministry of Fire Prevention.
```

### T7 Instrumental people / discard

- **Pathology:** Allies and voters are single-use tools; credit stolen, blame dumped
- **Herd misread:** "He surrounds himself with the best people until they let him down"
- **Satire engine:** Day-one onboarding includes the future betrayal press release

**Rules:** MUST show the discard as pre-scheduled. NEVER imply the junior clerk actually failed.

### T10 Loyalty tests that purge conscience

- **Pathology:** Force staff to defend an obvious lie; anyone with integrity leaves
- **Herd misread:** "A tight, loyal team of experts"
- **Satire engine:** Job interview is signing that a red wall is green

**Rules:** MUST make the lie visible to the viewer. NEVER frame sycophancy as competence.

## Psychopathy

### T2 Superficial charm mistaken for empathy

- **Pathology:** Camera-on warmth, camera-off contempt; love-bomb then discard
- **Herd misread:** "He is so relatable; he cares"
- **Satire engine:** Kiss the baby for the flash, tax the daycare before it cools

**Rules:** MUST switch the mask inside one panel. NEVER write genuine care with a cynical caption.

Gold: `content/cognitive-memetics/reptilocracy/2026-08-09-charisma-on-empathy-off/`

### T5 Zero remorse mistaken for toughness

- **Pathology:** No guilt, shame, or anxiety after crushing other people
- **Herd misread:** "Steady nerves; the hard calls keep him up at night"
- **Satire engine:** Calm lunch over a ruined province; clerks praise resilience

**Rules:** MUST show the missing guilt circuit, not "he is brave." NEVER invent a private tear the public cannot see.

### T6 Pathological lying mistaken for changing circumstances

- **Pathology:** Speech is a tool for the next five minutes; no attachment to yesterday
- **Herd misread:** "There must be two sides; why would he make that up?"
- **Satire engine:** Burning building labeled swimming pool; ants squint for nuance

**Rules:** MUST put the contradiction in the same frame. NEVER let the clerk "reconcile" the two statements into one truth.

Gold: `content/cognitive-memetics/reptilocracy/2026-09-27-the-bridge-is-open/`

### T8 Impulse / boredom mistaken for disruption

- **Pathology:** Under-aroused nervous system; peace is boring, so he blows up working systems
- **Herd misread:** "Bold disruptor shaking up a stale system"
- **Satire engine:** All gauges green; he pulls the DO NOT TOUCH lever for a pulse

**Rules:** MUST show the working system before he breaks it. NEVER credit strategy for a boredom fidget.
