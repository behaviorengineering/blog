# Reptilocracy Haiku candidates (Claude Haiku 4.5 thinking)

DESCRIPTION_1:
A crocodile eats lunch in the rubble of a bridge he sold for scrap while two frog clerks praise his grace under pressure. When one asks why he sold it, the other says not during lunch, because that would be uncivil.

DESCRIPTION_2:
Over the crocodile's lunch in the wreckage of the bridge he sold for scrap, two frog clerks log his grace under pressure. Asked why he sold it, one clerk says not during lunch, that would be uncivil.

DESCRIPTION_3:
Ants haul forms through the wreckage while a crocodile eats lunch on the bridge he sold for scrap. Frog clerks call his missing remorse grace under pressure, and when one asks why he sold it, the other says not during lunch, that would be uncivil.

TLDR:
The clerks read zero remorse as steady nerves, so a bridge sold for scrap passes for composure. The ministry records calm and etiquette, while psychological fitness, accountability, and remorse never reach the agenda.

FLUFF:
The bridge is rubble now. The crocodile eats his lunch in it with a napkin tucked into his collar, and the ants carry their forms across the wreckage one at a time because the forms still need stamping on the far side.

The frog clerks write up the meeting. The minutes record a calm lunch, good manners, and a steady hand, and the afternoon agenda has no item about the bridge.

Nobody asks why it went for scrap. The ants reroute through the river, the crocodile takes a second helping, and the clerks log another day of grace under pressure.
