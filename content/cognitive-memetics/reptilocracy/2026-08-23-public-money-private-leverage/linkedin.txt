"Public Money, Private Leverage"

❓ Tax money enters at one end and comes back as lobbying power at the other. The public funds the loop, then meets it again at the ballot box.

AND:
Incentives can build a closed loop between public money, private advantage, and political pull. A small group has the time, skill, and cash to chase the next exception. Everyone else eats a cost spread so thin that almost nobody tracks the transfer. They dress the gears in subsidy, partnership, competitiveness, reform. It can look professional even when public money helps pay for the influence that protects the payout. The smaller the group that wins, the louder it comes back to press again. Without honest, independently watched conflict-of-interest rules, the machine keeps paying the people who keep the gears turning.
