title: The Process Is Blamed
type: sayings
description: "When the room answers with a **diagram** instead of a **name**, **responsibility** thins until **no one owns it** but **everyone defends it**."
tldr: |
  We ask **who caused it**, and the room points to a **diagram**. **Responsibility** dissolves into systems that **no one owns** and everyone defends.
fluff: |
  This is **diluted ownership** across time and structure; decisions accumulate through many hands until **no single person** feels accountable. Committees, legacy systems, and inherited procedures make each step seem reasonable, so the outcome feels like **"just how the process works."**

  **System abstraction** reinforces it: we describe failure in abstract system terms instead of concrete decisions. This protects status and reduces personal risk, so it feels professional. **"If the system did it, no one did."** Without filters for honesty and accountability, systems promote people who **explain outcomes without owning them.**
