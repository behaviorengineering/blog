  **Narrative control** emerges inside the organisation: signals get translated into safer language before they move upward. This feels professional because it protects relationships and status. **"Bad news shrinks as it climbs."** Without filters for honesty, courage, and reality-contact, systems select people who **manage perception** instead of confronting risk early.
