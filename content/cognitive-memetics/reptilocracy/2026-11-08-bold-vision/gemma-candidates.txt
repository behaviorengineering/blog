# Reptilocracy Gemma candidates (thinking_chars=0)

DESCRIPTION_1:
The Komodo leader announces gravity is now optional for loyal citizens, and the clerks immediately begin debating the font size for the new laws of physics. They are too busy applauding his bold vision to notice the total collapse of reality.

DESCRIPTION_2:
When the Great Leader declares that gravity is a mere suggestion, the administration focuses on the implementation timeline rather than his connection to reality. It turns out high-level ambition is a perfect substitute for psychological fitness.

DESCRIPTION_3:
The frogs and ants applaud a speech that defies the laws of nature, mistaking a complete break from reality for a revolutionary roadmap. In this office, we budget for the logistics of delusions, but never for a sanity check.

TLDR:
Ambition is often just a grandiose delusion with a better marketing budget. The institution treats a leader's break from reality as a "bold vision" while ignoring the lack of psychological fitness. We manage the logistics of the impossible instead of questioning the person ordering it.

FLUFF:
The agenda is packed with implementation timelines, footnote styles, and rollout schedules for the new physics. No one mentions the fact that the man at the podium is currently hallucinating a world where gravity is a choice. To the clerks, a leader’s descent into narcissism is simply a sign of a forward-thinking mind.

We have excellent protocols for managing the fallout of a decree, but we have no protocols for managing the man making it. The institution is designed to absorb the shock of a meltdown by rebranding it as a strategic pivot.

The budget is allocated for the roadmap and the commemorative banners. There is no line item for a psychiatrist, because in the Reptilocracy, we don't check for fitness; we just check if the stamp hits the paper on time.
