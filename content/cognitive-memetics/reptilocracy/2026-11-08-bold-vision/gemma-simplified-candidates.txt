DESCRIPTION_1:
The High Minister declares gravity is now optional for the loyal, while the clerks spend the afternoon arguing over whether the decree should be set in Bold or Italic.

DESCRIPTION_2:
A new decree promises to abolish physics for the faithful, yet the bureaucratic machine only pauses to debate the margin width of the announcement.

DESCRIPTION_3:
The Council celebrates a bold vision of weightlessness, ignoring the fact that the only thing actually drifting is the leader's grip on reality.

TLDR:
The institution treats a sudden break from physics as a mere matter of font choice. We mistake a leader's grand delusion for a strategic pivot because we never bothered to check if they were sane.

FLUFF:
The decree arrived on heavy parchment, promising that gravity is no longer a requirement for those with sufficient patriotism. The Minister smiled, the cameras rolled, and the populace cheered for the sudden lightness of being.

In the basement offices, the clerks did not look up at the ceiling. They did not check the Minister's pulse or his grasp on the physical world. They simply debated whether the footnote regarding terminal velocity looked better in 10-point Serif or Sans-Serif.

The system does not require a sanity check, only a high-quality stamp. As long as the decree is formatted correctly, the descent into the abyss is considered a successful implementation of the vision.
