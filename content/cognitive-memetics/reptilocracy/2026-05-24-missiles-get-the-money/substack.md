## The ceiling is full of funded weapons

The money goes to **missiles**. That is not a metaphor about one budget line. It is the **allocation logic**: **visible power** wins checks, and the slow, messy damage in people's heads becomes **someone else's problem**.

## What counts as urgent

We treat **hardware** as urgent **security**. We treat **human damage** as a later, optional **repair**. **Short-term fear** drives it. Leaders fund what they can **point at** on a **podium**, not what quietly prevents harm later.

## The rigged game

This is a **rigged game**. Money flows to what looks **powerful**, what wins **contracts**, what sounds good in a speech. **Mental health support** gets pushed aside. Around here, **serious leadership** means bigger arsenals, not taking care of the people using them.

We insure the **weapons**, not the people who carry them. No filter for **empathy**, no honesty about the **cost**, no guts to back **invisible work**.

> More **hardware**. More damage left for later.
