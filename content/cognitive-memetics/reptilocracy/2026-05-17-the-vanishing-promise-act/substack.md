## Now you see the promise. Now you don't?

Look: on the campaign trail the **campaign promise** goes into the hat and never comes back out. The **trick** is not hiding the lie. It is making us feel **silly** for believing they would follow through.

## Show politics, not follow-through

Pure show politics. They climb on **big promises**, then twist them, kick them down the road, or "forget" them once they are in office. Short attention spans, news that moves on, and nobody owning the follow-up turn pledges into **props**, not commitments.

## When the promise breaks

If something goes wrong? They hunt for a **loophole** in what they said, blame something else, or offer half a gesture. In politics, promises expire faster than the applause that made them.

> The system promotes people who sell smoke, not people who keep their word.
