## Our values, but not like that

This week **management** refreshes **Our Values**: **think big**, **collaborate often**, but *don't go overboard*. On the slide those lines sound like **inspiration**. In the pasture they land like a **warning siren**.

> Nothing feels more corporate than values that glow in a deck, until someone tries to live them.

## When culture has a volume knob

The herd is not allergic to **ambition** or **teamwork**. They are allergic to the **fine print** tucked inside every virtue. **Innovate**, but not enough to **spook** the budget. **Collaborate**, but not so loud the calendar **bucks**. **Think big**, then **moderate** so nobody has to change a process.

Office language trains you to applaud the **verbs** and dodge the **limits**. The same poster that says *we rise together* can mean *rise, but not past your lane*.

## Why the cows keep a stopwatch

**Tales from the Cube Farm** is a small count of how many of these strips we can ship before **robots** read as just another ordinary coworker. Workplaces spent decades asking people to act like **reliable machinery** long before real machines could replace them. The **cube-cows** flip the joke: domesticated office life, drawn plain, with one quiet question about whether humans still want a little **wild individuality** before the actual robots fully move in.
