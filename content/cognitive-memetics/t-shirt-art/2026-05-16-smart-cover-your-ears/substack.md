## When your body moves before your head does

You know that feeling where your body reacts before you can explain what happened? I mean it in a **we-all-do-this** way, not a gotcha about bad people online.

It is not always a rough day on the apps. Picture the **family thread**, or the group chat where everyone already picked a side. Sometimes you jump in to back your people before you really check the fact, because the **sting shows up first**.

## Two alarms at once

Often **two things** hit together. **Reality** rubs against the story you already tell about the world, and something in the same moment feels like a threat to your crew or the face you show people. Your brain reads that mix as **danger** long before it feels like plain information.

## The easy move is to turn it down

So the first move usually is not careful analysis. It is scroll past, change the subject, **turn the volume down**. Quick relief. Naming what happened comes later, if it comes at all.

When **identity** is on the line, you push back to feel less exposed, not only to win the argument. We tweak the narrative before we update the map. That is human, not a character flaw.

> The Sm(art) T-shirt art says it like a serious joke: two alarms at once, and the easy shortcut is to mute the signal instead of sorting it.

**How you respond is still your call.** It still helps to notice what is running underneath.
