### The High Cost of Gravity

Trying to bury a fact is like trying to hold a beach ball underwater; it does not change the ball's nature, it just exhausts your arms. Eventually, the energy needed to maintain the illusion becomes too much to sustain. The truth breaks the surface on its own terms. You reach for it when the narrative starts cracking under its own weight.
