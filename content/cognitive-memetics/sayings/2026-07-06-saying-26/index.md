### The Cost of Hovering

Trust is a resource that evaporates the moment you start herding competent people. When you treat experts like sheep, you lose their initiative and inherit their silence. This micro-management creates resentment and stifles innovation, as capable individuals disengage from a process where their expertise is not valued.

You reach for it when a **nervous stakeholder** tries to dictate the exact steps of a process you have successfully run for years.
