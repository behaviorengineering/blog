### The Cost of the Fifth Leg

When you spin elaborate theories to defend a bad project, you are not saving face. Instead, you burn through your team's patience. The moment you start inventing extra limbs for your story, people stop listening to the explanation and start measuring your desperation. You feel the quiet resentment build as they wait for the next impossible excuse, and your word loses all weight.

You reach for it when a simple 'I got this wrong' would save your reputation, but pride makes you build a monster instead.
