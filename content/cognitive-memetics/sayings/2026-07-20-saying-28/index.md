### The Trap of the Low Ceiling

Mistaking the "least lost" person for a visionary caps a team's potential at their limited horizon. The stakes are not just mediocrity; it is the inevitable crash when the team faces a real, complex obstacle that requires absolute mastery, not just relative survival.

You reach for it when a team celebrates a basic fix as a stroke of genius, ignoring the fact that the entire system is still running in the dark.
