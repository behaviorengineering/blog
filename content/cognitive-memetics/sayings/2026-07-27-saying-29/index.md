### The Compounding Cost of "No"

When we resist minor friction, we are not escaping the load; we are just letting the interest accumulate. Stubbornness turns a tiny inconvenience into a full-blown crisis. This breeds resentment among those who then shoulder the doubled burden. Recognizing the small ask as a chance to avoid a much larger, more painful obligation is key.

You reach for it when a team member tries to shirk a basic administrative handoff, and you need to remind them that the clean-up crew charges double.
