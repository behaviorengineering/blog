"Tanto nadar para morir en la orilla."

❓ This saying is about months of steady work that dies right at the finish. Picture a team that spends six months on a pitch, works nights, and sends the final deck the morning of the meeting. Then they realize the client pulled the budget the day before signing.

AND:
Your uncle asks how the news landed over the rice. Before you can answer, your cousin asks someone to pass the hot sauce. Nobody circles back, so you keep eating without having to explain. Later, your aunt slides the last plantain onto your plate without a word, which is about as close to a hug as this table gets.

🔤 IN ENGLISH:
• To fall at the final hurdle
• So close, yet so far
• To miss by a whisker
• To snatch defeat from the jaws of victory
