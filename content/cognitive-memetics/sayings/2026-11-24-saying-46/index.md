### The morning of the meeting

The team sends the deck while the office is still half dark. Then someone opens the mail: the **client pulled the budget** yesterday. The meeting is still on the calendar, but the **yes is already gone**.

You reach for it when a friend says they came so close, and the other side had already decided.
