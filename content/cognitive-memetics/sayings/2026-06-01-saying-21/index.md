### The Art of Coexistence

The image is simple: **juntos** is the shared fish tank; **no revueltos** means keeping your own school. Fish can swim in the same water, cross paths, and share space, yet still maintain their distinct shapes and trajectories. A blender, on the other hand, trades that legibility for one uniform soup. Socially, this demands a specific kind of closeness: **near**, **legible**, and **accountable**.

You reach for it when proximity is real but **fusion** would cost trust.
