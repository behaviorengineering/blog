### The hand on the lid pays too

The saying warns about **borrowed consequences**. Someone else may have caused the problem, but the person who hides it helps keep the pressure inside. When the truth comes out, that helper is already close enough to get burned.

You reach for it when a coworker covers for a manager, a friend keeps quiet about a bad decision, or a family member takes responsibility for damage they did not cause.
