"El que tapa la olla también se quema."

❓ The lid looks like protection until the steam reaches the hand holding it down. You use this saying when someone helps hide a fault and ends up paying for the consequences too: a junior lies to a manager about a senior's missed deadline, then gets written up when the story breaks.

AND:
It is the wary shrug you give a friend who volunteers to cover someone else's mess. A coworker deletes the warning email, smooths over a missed payment, or tells the client that the delay is under control. When the pot boils over, the person who kept the lid on is standing closest to the steam.

• If you play with fire, you'll get burned
• If you lie down with dogs, you get up with fleas
• You reap what you sow
