### The notebook tax on every retelling

Truth lets you forget the details. A lie makes you inventory them. Every new audience gets a private version, and the liar pays rent in mental energy just to keep the seams from showing.

You reach for it when someone cannot keep their own cover story straight across two meetings, or when a public figure's old quote finally collides with today's press release.
