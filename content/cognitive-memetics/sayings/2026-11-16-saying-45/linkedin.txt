"Tené buena memoria, sobre todo si eres mentiroso."

It is the nervous flip through a notebook you picture when a friend has to reconstruct a lie on the spot. You hear it after someone asks, "Wait, didn't you say Tuesday?" and the speaker freezes long enough for everyone to hear the gears grinding.
