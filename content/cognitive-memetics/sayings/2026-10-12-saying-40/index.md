### When Credibility Goes Bankrupt

A history of deceit means your word holds no weight. Even when you speak plainly, people file it as a tactical play. This is draining. No one buys what you are selling, and they dismiss your genuine honesty.

You would use this when a team member tries to rebuild trust but finds they are still paying interest on old debts.
