### The Compounding Cost of the Cover-Up

When you fabricate a quick fix, you inherit the exhausting job of maintaining it. The real stakes are not just getting caught. You're constantly looking over your shoulder to keep the story straight, and you lose everyone's trust the moment reality hits.

You reach for it when someone tries to patch a **structural crack** with a quick excuse, forgetting that reality always outlasts the spin.
