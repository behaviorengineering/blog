### The Cost of the Cute Shrug

When someone plays innocent about their own actions, they are not just dodging a mistake. They transfer the mental and emotional burden of the cleanup to you. Trust frays because you cannot solve a problem with someone who pretends they were not even in the room when it happened. You feel the urge to confront them when the evidence is staring you both in the face, but they are still waiting for you to buy their act.
