### The Cost of the Cute Shrug

When someone plays the innocent bystander to their own actions, they transfer the cognitive and emotional load of the cleanup to you. Trust frays because you cannot solve a problem with someone who pretends they were not even in the room when it happened.

You reach for it when the evidence is staring you both in the face, but they are still waiting for you to buy the act.
