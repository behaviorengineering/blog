### In English

> Closest English equivalents include:
>
> - *They won't lift a finger and act like a dog in the manger.*
> - *They don't help and they get in the way.*
> - *They're useless and obstructive.*

### What the double hit costs

When someone monopolizes the shared tool, the rest of the house or team burns time hunting a workaround. You end up doing their share plus yours. The saying names that double hit: idle hands, and a locked cupboard.

True trust in a kitchen, a family, or an office needs people who either wash or pass the tub. Holding both jobs hostage trains everyone else to stop asking and start resenting.
