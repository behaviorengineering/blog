### The Question He Cannot Answer

The coworker gets away with it until a client asks a question he cannot answer. He looks at the intern. The intern answers in two sentences, and the boss sees, maybe for the first time, who has been **carrying the work**.

You reach for it when your boss praises the new guy's polish while you're still fixing his slides.
