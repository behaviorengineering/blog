"La mona, aunque se vista de seda, mona se queda."

❓ It describes people who want to pretend they are something they are not. They put on the silk, try and try and try, and they are still the same old monkey. A coworker shows up with a new car, then tailored suits, then a new title, and still hands the real work to the interns. When a deadline slips, the intern stays late and takes the client's complaint while the coworker collects the credit.

AND:
It is the tired shrug of someone who has watched the same person try again and again and come out unchanged. You say it about the cousin who announces a new venture every year and has never paid anyone back. Nobody argues, because everyone has already seen the last three versions.

🔤 IN ENGLISH:
• All hat and no cattle
• Lipstick on a pig
• You can't make a silk purse out of a sow's ear
