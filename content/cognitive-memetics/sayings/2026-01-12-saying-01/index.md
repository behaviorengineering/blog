title: "Tú sabes más que pescao frito"
type: sayings
description: "Affectionate awe for the friend who **spots the trap** before you do."
tldr: |
  You're so **street-sharp** that nothing slips past you. The joke compares that "knowing" to the taste of fried fish, because **saber** in Spanish can mean both **to know** and **to taste**.

  People say it like "**sharp as a tack**" or "**too smart for your own good**," with **affection** and joking awe.
fluff: |
  It is the **grin** you give the friend who saw the angle before anyone else. You use it when someone **out-negotiates** a seller, **dodges** a scam, or reads a room faster than you. You also hear it as warm teasing: praise wrapped in fried-fish exaggeration, not a cold insult.
