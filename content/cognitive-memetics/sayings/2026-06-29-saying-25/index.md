### The High Cost of Cold Feet

Backing out of a shared commitment late in the game triggers a collapse. Others must absorb the wreckage of your abandoned share. This creates resentment and can unravel trust.

You reach for it when someone tries to pull the plug on a project after the team has already crossed the **point of no return**.
