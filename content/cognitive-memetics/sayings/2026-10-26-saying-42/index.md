### The Tax on Polite Lies

When you sugarcoat reality to avoid immediate awkwardness, you do not save the relationship. You just shift the debt to the future. More than hurt feelings are at stake. You also risk the slow, quiet rot of trust. People realize you would let them walk off a cliff just to keep a meeting pleasant. The team loses faith in your judgment because you prioritize comfort over truth.

You reach for it when you must break the comfortable silence and say what everyone thinks but is too terrified to admit.
