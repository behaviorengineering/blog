### The Cost of Cheap Bones

When you accept a transparently bad excuse, you are not being polite. You are training people that you are cheap to buy off. They throw you a dry bone (a rushed text, a vague delay, a friend constantly flaking on plans with flimsy reasons) because it takes less effort than delivering real results or telling the hard truth. Every time you nod and swallow it to avoid a confrontation, **resentment builds**, and the quality of your relationships degrades. True trust requires holding the line on reality, not accepting cheap pretexts.

You reach for this saying when someone tries to insult your intelligence with a pretext so thin you can see right through it.
