### The Cost of the Tiny Band-Aid

When a manager offers a $15 gift card to resolve **team-wide burnout**, the team does not feel appreciated. They feel insulted, and trust completely evaporates. This small gesture, meant to "fix" a huge problem, only highlights how little leadership understands or cares. A cover-up this small only highlights the disaster's true scale.
