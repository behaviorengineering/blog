  November 1963.

  Lyndon Johnson became president in sudden trauma. He had survived a heart attack in 1955. There was no public assessment of whether he was fit to lead during a Cold War **crisis**.

  The full extent of his health problems became clear only years later. The public trusted the office. They did not know the state of the man.

  Clear frameworks for fitness rebuild public confidence in how **decisions** are made.
