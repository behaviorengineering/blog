  July 1985.

  Reagan underwent intestinal surgery and invoked the Twenty-fifth Amendment. Vice President Bush held presidential powers for about eight hours while doctors assessed his condition. Reagan resumed duties that evening under explicit medical oversight.

  Political strength is not silence under pain. It is knowing when you need support, and building a system that provides it.

  We need to update our definition of what strength looks like in **leadership**.
