This site is a place where I store my thoughts.

I like big platforms, but I do not trust them as an archive.

When they mix with hidden interests and incentives that trap you, they can stop feeling like an extension of yourself and start feeling like borrowed space. If you depend on that space, you are exposed to sudden changes. Feeds change, accounts vanish, and “search” is not a promise.

So this is my shelf. If you are early, you are seeing the workshop.

I hope you find amusement, insights, and every now and then a bit of discomfort. Truth is supposed to feel that way to our biological brains. Happy discovery.

## What you will find here

- Essays about behavior, shared reality, and how systems steer people.
- Curated videos that changed how I think.
- Experiments. Some will work. Some will be ugly.

## What to expect

- I will keep uploading and reorganizing.
- Posts will range from quick notes to polished pieces.
- If something feels unfinished, it probably is.

## If you want the best entry points

Start with the latest posts on the home page, then follow sections that match your mood.
