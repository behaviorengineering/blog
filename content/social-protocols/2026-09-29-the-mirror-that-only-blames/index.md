A vampire checks the mirror after a feeding. The glass stays empty. Without a reflection to show the blood or the hunger, the vampire looks around for the source of the mess. The blank surface directs the fault toward the furniture, the lighting, or the people standing nearby.

This **blindness functions as a closed circuit**. When a person cannot register their own image, they **lose the ability to locate fault within themselves**. They search for a problem and find only a **void where accountability should live**. This void **pushes blame outward**. Every mistake becomes a problem caused by the environment or the people in it. **Everyone nearby becomes a target**.

### The menu that omits your own name

This logic dictates how a person explains their life. During a crisis, they consult a **mental menu of possible causes** to find a culprit. This menu **omits their own name**. A person with this blindness treats their own **agency as a non-option**. They scan the faces of colleagues or family members to find the source of the friction, identifying every **external reaction as the primary cause**.

### How bystanders learn to freeze

The people standing nearby quickly learn how to **survive the menu**. They realize that pointing out the mess only makes them the next target. To keep the peace, **bystanders freeze**. They swallow their objections, manage the fallout, and quietly clean up the damage so the person never has to notice it.

### The heavy price of a blank mirror

This strategy exacts a **heavy psychological toll**. A silent witness spends their days holding their breath, constantly calculating how to **absorb the blame** without triggering another crisis. They become an **exhausted surface**, polished flat by the effort of reflecting nothing back. The tragedy is that this **quiet endurance only feeds the original blindness**.

The **cost of the loop** is paid entirely by those around them. When people choose silence to avoid conflict, they inadvertently **build the very glass the blind person needs to stay blind**. Every unaddressed outburst or uncorrected lie becomes proof that the world is at fault. **Accountability requires a witness who refuses to be a blank surface**. You cannot fix a missing reflection by hiding the damage.
