🤝 Why does conquest start with a partnership?

Look: in this lecture, Rome does not kick the door in first. It sends an invite.

Ever been invited as a "partner" and somehow you were the one paying for the next expansion? That is the trick.

You keep your laws, your mayor, your name on the shop. You just send troops (or recruits) for their next campaign. It still feels like partnership. The bill sits with you.

Johnathan Bi reading Machiavelli: occupation is expensive and people rebel. A fake alliance scales. When you have done the dirty work, you stop being a partner and start being a subject.

Same smile on a sales pyramid. You keep your pitch. The upline lives off the people you pay to find.

If you still have the title and someone else eats the spoils, you are the junior partner in their conquest.
