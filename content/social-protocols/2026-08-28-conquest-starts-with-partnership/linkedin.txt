🤝 Why does conquest start with a partnership?

Johnathan Bi on Machiavelli: Rome did not open with occupation. It opened with an invite.

✜ Junior partner, not equal
Nearby cities kept their laws and rulers. They supplied troops for Rome's next war. The public story was partnership. The military fact was subservience.

✜ Force costs too much
A league of equals stalls in committee. Direct occupation burns treasure and breeds rebellion. Fraud scales: keep the costume of alliance, shift the bill.

✜ Then the invite is rank
Rome used those partners to take the next cities, then turned the partners into subjects. The center that still looks kind keeps the ring.

A sales pyramid runs the same move. You keep your name and pitch. The upline lives off the recruits you pay to find.

If you keep your laws, logo, or title while someone else sets the next campaign and eats the spoils, you are the junior partner in someone else's conquest. The fraud is the operating system.
