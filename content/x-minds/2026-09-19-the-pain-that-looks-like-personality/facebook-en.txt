Ever get wrecked by a period in a text?

Your partner drops a full stop instead of an emoji and shame hits your chest like a punch. Heart racing. Worthlessness flooding in. That spike has a name: Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD). The brake that should slow the surge just… does not show up.

A teenager crashes after a breakup and gets labeled with a personality disorder. Heavy shame. Then ADHD comes into view and the story changes: the brain is missing the pause signals. Not a broken character. A missing brake.

Some people bounce between antidepressants that never quite land because the real gap is dopamine and noradrenaline. Spot ADHD-linked RSD and treatments like Guanfacine can help build that pause. When the spikes finally quiet, it feels less like “who I am” and more like chemistry that can be managed.

Or picture a vague “let’s talk Monday” from your boss. Whole weekend gone. Naming it “this is my RSD” opens a tiny gap so you can think again before the landslide.

Next time the wave hits, try that label out loud. One second of space between trigger and spike is already a win.
