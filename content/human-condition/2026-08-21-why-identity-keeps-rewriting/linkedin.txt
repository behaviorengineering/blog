🪞 Why does the brain keep selling you a new "me"?

Identity feels like a sealed file. Heather Berlin, Ethan Kross, and Nicole Vignola argue it is a live build: the brain mixes what it already expects with what the senses pick up now, then sells the blend as you.

✜ You pull the evidence your mood already wants
Mindset steers what you notice in the same room. A negative headspace mines for negative proof. You live inside a perception box: a bounded window you treat as the whole world.

✜ The voice writes the plot, then it can loop
Your inner voice helps you plan and turn events into a story of who you are. When it loops as chatter (rumination and catastrophizing), the same tool traps you.

✜ Self-criticism starts as copying
You copy what you see in parents and peers, not only what you are told. Bad news lands harder than good, so those early stories harden if nobody checks them.

Name the box they put you in, and the box you put yourself in. Then rewrite it. Other people read the rewrite too.
