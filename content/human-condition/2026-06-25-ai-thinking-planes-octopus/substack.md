## You think you think?

People say AI is **taking our thinking**. That is partly right. It may only be right on the *habit plane*. We still have to think; we just have to do it on the **metacognition plane**.

> We still have to think; we just have to do it on the **metacognition plane**.

## When "thinking" is someone else's script

A lot of what we call thinking right now is **following someone else's thinking** without giving it real thought. Opinions arrive pre-packaged. Reactions fire before you ask why. Mental habits run like **muscle memory**.

In that sense we are less like a single, unified mind and more like an **octopus** with **local intelligence**.

## Arms that think locally

An octopus does not keep all of its intelligence in one central brain. Its **arms** have their own **dense clusters of neurons**. They can explore, grasp, and decide almost independently, while the main brain only gets involved when it really matters.

> **Segmented local control**: the central brain sets goals while the limbs handle sensorimotor detail.

Our days work the same way. The routine **arms** of the day (scrolling, reacting, following routines, letting AI draft and sort and summarize) run on *local programs*. They do not need much **conscious oversight**.

## Artificial tentacles

We hand some of those tasks to **artificial tentacles**: recommendation systems, large language models, planning tools. They are **extra arms** reaching into the world on our behalf.

That shifts work from mechanical execution to **choosing among many drafts at once**. A designer who once spent nine hours manually moving elements on a screen now lets the limbs move the pieces at scale. The effort remains, but the mind evaluates a dozen **proofs-of-concept** simultaneously. 

> You work at the **speed of selection**.

The tragedy is treating every arm as the whole mind, then wondering why nothing inside feels awake.

## Arm moving vs mind waking up

There is still a difference between an *arm moving* and a *mind waking up*.

You can let your human and artificial arms run while you sit back and watch TV, without a thought. Or you can become more like the **octopus brain** watching its own limbs: track which arms you **outsourced** and which you abandoned.

**Thinking ahead of your thoughts** means directing this parallel spread. You set the target, let the artificial limbs map the territory, and select the pattern that hits the mark.
