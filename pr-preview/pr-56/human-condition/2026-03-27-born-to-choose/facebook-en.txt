🔥🎯 When stakes run high, choice stops feeling free

Ever notice you still want to pick, but you are not really picking anymore?

When the stakes climb, you stop hunting for the best option and start hunting for relief. The phone keeps serving plans, products, and feeds. You tap the default, or you let someone you trust decide, as long as you can still tell yourself, "I'm in control."

Politics works the same way. We vote. Then the specialists and institutions carry the weight. We keep the feeling that we chose. They carry the work and the blame when it goes wrong.

The dark end of this is people with almost no fear, guilt, or regret. Everyone else freezes on the crisis call. They move. Systems that reward delegation start treating that coldness like a superpower, until the numbness that kills anxiety also kills empathy and correction.

The part that stays yours is noticing when you handed the choice off.
