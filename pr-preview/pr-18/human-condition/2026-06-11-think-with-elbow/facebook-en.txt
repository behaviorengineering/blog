💆💭 Can you feel yourself thinking?

Ever try to notice where a thought "starts"? You touch a table and the solid feel sits on your hand, out in the world. You never feel your skull buzzing.

When you think, the "you" that thinks gets glued behind your eyes. Your knee does not get credit for the plan. Your hand does not feel like it is thinking.

Same brain wiring both times. Different felt address. And because you never feel the switch, the story just feels obvious.

Press your elbow on the desk and try to make the next thought begin in the joint. Pressure, yes. Words, still behind your eyes. The elbow has nothing to say.

In people who are blind or deafblind, touch and body sense do more of the work. The felt home of thinking might drift from the head toward the torso or the whole body. Nobody has measured that directly yet, but the pattern fits.

Same machine. Two locations. One sounds like plain truth.
