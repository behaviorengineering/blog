😫🔄 Ever notice the loudest person in your feed is not always the one who knows the most?

Look: there is this cycle people share like ancient wisdom. "Hard times create strong men, strong men create good times, good times create weak men, and weak men create hard times." It is from a 2016 novel, not a proverb.

Hard times forge capable people. They build stable systems. Prosperity lets the wrong people rise; they run things badly and set up the next crash.

Now add the algorithms. Feeds rank by clicks and watch time. Outrage and self-promotion spike those signals. Showboats beat builders. Search and recommendations follow the loudest voice.

Same old cycle. Just faster.
