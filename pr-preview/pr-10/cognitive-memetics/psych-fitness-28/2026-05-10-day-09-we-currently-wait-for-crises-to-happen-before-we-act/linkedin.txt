✔️ THE FACTS:

April 1986.

Chernobyl's reactor four exploded. Pripyat was evacuated within roughly thirty-six hours, but officials downplayed the severity to their own population and to neighboring countries for days.

Denial is a psychological defense mechanism. In leaders under pressure, it becomes dangerous. They deliberately held back as the disaster unfolded.

A system that checks psychological fitness can catch these patterns early. It supports leaders before denial hardens into policy.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.
