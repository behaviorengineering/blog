✔️ THE FACTS:

June 2010.

Kevin Rudd was removed as prime minister and replaced by Julia Gillard. Three years later Gillard was removed and Rudd returned. In September 2013 Tony Abbott won government, the third prime minister in three years.

Voters watched leadership lurch from crisis to crisis.

Reactive politics exhausts the public and erodes trust. Leaders with support structures can pause, consult, and choose deliberately instead of governing from permanent emergency.

Supported leaders can pause and choose wisely.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.

❓ THE STAKES:

This is Day 23 of 28 in a series counting down the human factors that shape high-stakes decisions. Cabinet ministers make choices affecting millions, yet they rarely face the fitness-for-duty scrutiny that pilots and surgeons accept as normal. The petition asks for stronger checks and balances on psychological fitness for Australia's top leaders because the stakes deserve the same rigor we demand in cockpits and operating rooms.
