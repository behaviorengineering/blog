"Más inútil que cenicero de moto."

❓ You put an ashtray on a speeding motorcycle; the wind makes it useless at the root. You use this expression when you want to say something adds nothing, for example when a project task is pointless busywork.

AND:
Some things are not just bad; they come failed from conception. The law nobody respects, the KPI everyone games, the call to conscience in a place where everyone looks out for themselves.
