"Juntos, pero no revueltos."

❓ The saying draws a line between sharing a space and losing your edges. Think of a fish tank: the fish share the water, but they do not merge into one school. It is not a blender, where everything melts into one soup. You use it when two crews share a party but stay in their huddle, or when tasks split clean so nobody quietly absorbs the whole load.

AND:
It lands as polite fence paint: warm, not cold. You hear it from the host sorting seating, from siblings sharing a flat, or from a lead who wants clear lanes on a board. It names the wish for harmony with names still legible.

🔤 IN ENGLISH:
• Together, not scrambled
• Each keeps its shape
• Same room, own lane
• Good fences make good neighbors
