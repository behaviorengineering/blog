✔️ THE FACTS:

April 1970.

Apollo 13 was roughly halfway to the moon when an oxygen tank ruptured. Carbon dioxide rose. Power dropped. One crew could not think their way home alone.

Mission control in Houston ran simulations around the clock. Checklists, backup plans, and teams on the ground made the return possible. The astronauts had skill. The system had depth.

We are halfway through this petition. Every signature today builds similar mechanisms for our leaders.
