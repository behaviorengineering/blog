💬 "Aquí el que no corre vuela." (Here, if you don't run, you fly.)

Ever hear a Venezuelan friend say that when someone beat them to the punch?

It does not mean "slow and steady wins." It means even running is not enough. You have to fly. It is the sigh of someone who finally gets how a broken system actually works: play by the polite rules and you stay grounded.

You say it when someone cuts the line during a shortage, or when you hesitate and the deal disappears. Advice, warning, and a little bitterness in one line.

The phrase is not asking you to grind harder. It is asking for agility, ingenuity, and sometimes... well, shortcuts.

The shrug of someone who already learned the game's real rules.
