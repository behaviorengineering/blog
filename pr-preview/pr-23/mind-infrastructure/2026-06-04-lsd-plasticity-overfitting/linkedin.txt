💊🌀 Why might someone pass exams on LSD without notes?

At uni a housemate told me about a friend who showed up to one class on LSD every time. No notebook, no laptop, yet he supposedly passed every exam. When psychedelic research went mainstream, that story finally got my attention again.

✜ Plasticity window
Classic psychedelics can open a brief neuroplasticity window: the brain rewires synapses more readily for hours or days after a dose.

✜ Next-day tradeoff
A low-dose LSD study, tested the next day, found better episodic memory and verbal fluency, but worse cognitive flexibility when rules changed without warning.

✜ Overfitting read
Recent inputs weigh too heavy: recall jumps, rule-switching sinks, like ML overfitting. A bias toward whatever is in front of you. No paper I cite uses that label for LSD cognition; analogy, not established science.

Same lecture, extra plasticity, almost obsessive focus on what's in front of you: you might stamp the material in with unusual strength. The note-less pass stops looking like pure magic. I never verified the story; speculative read, not a recommendation.
