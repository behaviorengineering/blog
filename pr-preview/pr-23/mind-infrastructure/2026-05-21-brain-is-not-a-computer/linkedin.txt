🧠 Our brain doesn't even fit in our brain

You simplify to think, you drop detail, draw a line around the problem, and accept a few small lies so you can hold it in your head. The trap is when you forget you made that cut and treat the slice as how reality works.

✜ Like becomes is
Brain talk spreads as short comparisons: tubes, telegraph wires, computers, prediction machines. Each retelling drops what does not fit the story: glia, chemistry, bodies, the world outside the skull. Repeat the slice often enough and the comparison becomes reality: the brain gets sold as a computer.

✜ Your tools set your brain story
Your mind-story follows whatever tool is on the bench (the tools and ideas you have on hand): today an LLM trained on huge written archives. The "prediction engine" brain story starts to sound like fact because the text it learned from comes back as smooth insight.

✜ Mechanism, story, or reassurance?
"How does the brain work?" depends on whether you need a mechanism, a fundable story, a roadmap, or reassurance. AGI hype narrows it to "are we on track?" and benchmarks that sound certain until the model leaves training and stumbles.

Before you repeat a frame, say what it is for and what it leaves out.
