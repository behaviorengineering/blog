😫🔄 Hard times, strong men, and feeds that reward noise over skill

"Hard times create strong men, strong men create good times, good times create weak men, and weak men create hard times."

That line is from G. Michael Hopf's 2016 novel Those Who Remain, not an ancient proverb. He wrote it after generational-cycle theory like Strauss and Howe's The Fourth Turning: stability, decay, crisis on an ~80-year loop.

✜ The cycle in plain English
Hard times forge capable people. Capable people build stable systems. Prosperity lets narcissistic, incompetent actors rise; they run things poorly and set up the next crash.

✜ What feeds do to the last step
Engagement-driven rankings reward clicks and watch time. Outrage and self-promotion spike those signals, so the system boosts shallow performers over builders. Influencer culture becomes a narcissism factory.

You see it in search and recommendations: loud creators outrank domain experts, and decisions follow the loudest voice. The weak-men phase gets shorter. The next crisis lands sooner.

Same feed. Faster collapse.
