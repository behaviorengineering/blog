The Mirror That Only Blames

A vampire checks the mirror after a feeding. The glass stays empty, so the mess must be the furniture, the light, or the people standing there.

That is the tell in real life. "I caused this" never makes the menu. Every crisis gets a culprit scan of colleagues, partners, or kids, and the nearest face wins.

✜ Bystanders learn fast. Point at the damage and you become the next target. People freeze, swallow objections, and quietly clean up so the blind person never has to see it.

✜ Silence is not neutral. Each quiet witness becomes another blank mirror, proof from the inside that nothing is wrong. The witness pays in held breath and polished flat exhaustion.

The loop is paid by everyone except the person who cannot reflect. Accountability needs someone who refuses to be a blank surface. You cannot fix a missing reflection by hiding the damage.

Who in your life keeps handing you the bill?
