🔥🎯 When stakes run high, choice stops feeling free

We say we love choice. Past a point it turns on us.

When stakes rise, you stop chasing the best option and start chasing relief from anxiety. You still want to feel in charge, even when you are not doing the choosing.

✜ The menu keeps growing: plans, products, feeds that never quit. You hand the call to defaults or experts and protect one story: "I am still in control."

✜ Law and politics copy the script. You vote. Specialists and institutions carry the load. You keep the story of agency. They carry the work and the blame when things go wrong.

✜ Psychopathic traits hack this setup. Low fear, low guilt, weak regret: the brakes that freeze most people are gone. Picture the crisis call where everyone else freezes; they move. In systems built on delegation and avoidance, that looks like an edge, until the same numbness that kills anxiety kills feedback, empathy, and correction.

Notice when you are handing the choice off. That part is still yours.
