🖐️🧠 You point first, then you think.

When people explain a route, they gesture before the words arrive. The body locks in the spatial map; speech queries it. Researchers found that sitting on your hands degrades direction-giving. The thought needs the movement.

✜ Spatial cognition uses roughly half the brain and evolved long before language. Words ride on top of a physical foundation.

✜ Blind people develop strong spatial reasoning using touch, sound, texture, and proprioception. Visual cortex areas repurpose for spatial and tactile processing. The underlying scaffold stays the same.

✜ Abstract concepts like justice grow from visceral roots. We balance our hands to show equality. Babies react bodily to unfairness before they have words for it.

✜ We may anchor memory in quasi-spatial directions, accessing the past or future by subtly orienting toward them.

Suppress movement and you remove the tool that builds the thought.
