🎭 Look, ever been somewhere everyone knows something's off and nobody says it?

At work, at home, in the group chat: someone says "it's for safety" or "to help people" and everyone nods, even though everyone smells something else.

That's the deniability hood. Nobody has to be fooled. Everyone just pretends to believe it. You think "I see it," but say it out loud and you're the problem.

Researchers ran a trust game: with cover, betrayal went from 54% to 88%.

If everyone's wearing the hood, calling someone out exposes you. Nobody moves until the doubt isn't yours alone anymore.
