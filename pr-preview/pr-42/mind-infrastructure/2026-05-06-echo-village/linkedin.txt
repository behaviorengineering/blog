🏕️ They rebuilt your village to keep you circling.

Your pocket feed is not a neutral stream.
It is a rebuilt village, designed to keep you on the same paths, with the same people, repeating the same stories.

✜ Identity
The system learns which labels you cluster around, region, role, vibe, behavior class.
Then it routes you back to people who reinforce that identity.

✜ Cohesion
It rewards dense ties and fast circulation.
The tighter the group wiring, the more the group feels like "home".

✜ Topic alignment
It watches how far you drift from the group's script.
If you diverge, it can treat that as a stability risk and pull you back toward on-script content.

Here is the trap.
A system can optimize for group stability without optimizing for truth.
A philosophy circle and a cult can score the same if both stay cohesive and on-topic.

Stability is not wisdom.
