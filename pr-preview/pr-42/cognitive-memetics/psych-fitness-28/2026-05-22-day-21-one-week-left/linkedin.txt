✔️ THE FACTS:

September 1919.

Woodrow Wilson collapsed on a national speaking tour for the League of Nations in Pueblo, Colorado. A major stroke followed days later. For months the country debated a treaty while access to the president was tightly controlled.

The Senate rejected the League in November. No one had assessed whether he was fit to keep negotiating.

Leaders need systems that support them at maximum pressure. One week remains to build that system.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.
