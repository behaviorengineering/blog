🕰️🤝 You sync meaning through shared time, not through the same vocabulary

Real communication is not a vocabulary drill. It is shared context, practice, and lived experience. If you skip the time where life actually overlaps, the same words quietly fork into different worlds.

✜ Alignment is a ladder, not a slogan. Close relationships build shared reality when shared feelings turn into shared practices, then shared coordination, then something like a shared sense of who "we" are. Skip the rungs and you still sound aligned while drifting apart.

✜ Language is a zip file for a whole inner universe. Someone else unzips it with a different brain. They never get your exact state, only their reconstruction.

✜ Every sentence is a meme: a compressed pattern their mind inflates into a story, feeling, or frame. Same string, different decompression.

✜ That is the tribe problem in plain clothes. Less shared life, more meaning drift. Same words, different worlds.

So if the goal is "communicate better", say what you are buying. Office days, perks, and forced socials do not purchase the thing. Low-agenda time together does, the kind where nobody is secretly keeping score.
