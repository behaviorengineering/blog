⚓ Ever say "I'm staying out of this"?

A new rule lands at work and nobody knows who it screws. The boss nods; in minutes everyone enforces what they criticized in private.

- Bystander: "I'll see what others do."
- Recruit: "These folks get it."
- Enforcer: "Just following orders."

You watch, cheer, sign; you feel more respectable and less like the call is yours.

You stand behind what you sign and draw a clear line.

Blurry rules, rank above you, and pressure that creeps up in silence keep nudging you from "I'm staying out of this" to "just following orders."
