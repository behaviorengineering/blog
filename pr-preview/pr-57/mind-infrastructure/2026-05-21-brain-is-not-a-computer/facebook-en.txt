🧠 Why does "the brain is a computer" sound obvious?

You drop detail, draw a line around the problem, and accept a few small lies so you can hold it in your head. The trap is when you forget you made that cut and treat the slice like the whole world.

Brain talk runs in shorthand: tubes, wires, computers, prediction machines. Each pass drops what does not fit. Repeat it enough and the comparison turns into fact.

Right now the tool on the bench is AI, so prediction starts to pass for understanding.

Before you repeat a frame, say what it is for and what it leaves out.
