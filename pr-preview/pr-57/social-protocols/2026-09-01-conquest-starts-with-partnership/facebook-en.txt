🤝 Why does conquest start with a partnership?

Look: in this lecture, Rome does not kick the door in first. It sends an invite.

Ever been invited as a "partner" and somehow you were the one paying for the next expansion? That is the trick.

You keep your laws, your mayor, your name on the shop. You just send troops (or recruits) for their next campaign. It still feels like partnership. The bill sits with you. That is proxy war with a friendly contract.

Johnathan Bi reading Machiavelli: occupation is expensive and people rebel. A fake alliance scales. When you have done the dirty work, you stop being a partner and start being a subject. After Sparta won a war, it collapsed trying to run an empire it was not built for.

Same move in a pyramid sales scheme: you keep your pitch, recruit the next ring, and the people above you live off that expansion.

Nukes make classic land-grabs suicidal, so the hunt shifts: buy companies, push tech, cheat investors instead of junior partners.

If you still have the title and someone else sets the next campaign, you are the junior partner in their conquest.
