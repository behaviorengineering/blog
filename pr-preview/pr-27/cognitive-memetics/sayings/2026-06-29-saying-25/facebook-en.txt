💬 "Chivo que se devuelve, se esnuca." (The goat that turns back breaks its neck.)

Ever been halfway through something big and felt the panic to reverse?

Picture a goat on a narrow cliff ledge. Going forward is scary. Turning around is worse. One wrong step going back and that is it.

Venezuelans pull this out when someone wants to drop out of a tough program in the final stretch, or when a partner tries to panic-revert to an old plan after the team already pivoted.

The line is brutal on purpose. Hesitation on the ledge can cost more than the climb. Bail late on a shared bet and other people absorb the wreckage you leave behind.

Sometimes the hardest move is not starting. It is not turning back once you are already out there.
