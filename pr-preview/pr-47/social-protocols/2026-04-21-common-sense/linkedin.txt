🧮 Common sense is Engineered

"Common sense" is not something you were born with.

It is something installed in you.

A 2024 study (Whiting & Watts, PNAS) asked 2,046 people to judge 4,407 statements and guess what "most people" would say about each one. A statement only counted as "common sense" when most people agreed and knew that others agreed too.

Outside basic physical facts, they found no universal consensus. Only small groups agreeing on narrow slices of reality. That kills the idea of a shared human instinct.

✜ Why does 2 + 2 = 4 feel obvious?

Not because it is natural. Because a system pushed the same math curriculum through millions of kids for a decade. The agreement was manufactured, then sold back to you as obvious.

When someone like Terrence Howard confidently insists that 1 × 1 = 2, you are not watching a broken person. You are watching a different protocol run in his head. His "obvious" is simply not ours.

✜ Where does the feeling of "obvious" come from?

School, media, work, and your social circle repeat some patterns and ignore others. Over time, those patterns harden into fast, automatic judgments. They stop feeling learned and start feeling like reality itself.

✜ Shared training is not the same as showing up

Common sense is not a global setting. It is a local protocol. It syncs best with people who share your training and your information bubble, but only if you actually interact with them.

When was the last time you had a good chat with your neighbours?
