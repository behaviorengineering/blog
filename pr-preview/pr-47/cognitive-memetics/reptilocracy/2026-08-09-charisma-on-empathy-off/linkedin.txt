"Charisma On, Empathy Off"

❓ On the table, they drain empathy out like excess fluid and tick the box for success. The checklist keeps charisma and party loyalty switched on, but turns off the part of us that hurts when others do.

AND:
Fear and incentives teach the same lesson: feel too much for the people who bear the cost and you slow the decision, resist the harsh call, or sit with guilt. So the culture splits the smile from the feeling. Charm and slogans stay; real concern goes numb. They rename the distance toughness, objectivity, or putting the mission first, and call the extraction professional maturity. When hurting less matters more than harming less, the system anesthetizes itself. Without filters for real empathy and the courage to act on it, we promote leaders who can smile while their decisions quietly grind other nervous systems down.
