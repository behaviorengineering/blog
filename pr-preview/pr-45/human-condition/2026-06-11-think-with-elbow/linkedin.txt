💆💭 Can you feel yourself thinking?

Touch and thought share one perceptual machine, but you get two felt assignments: the world on your hand, the operator in your head.

Your brain runs the show. You never feel it working. It hides behind a clean story.

✜ Touch →
The world sits on your skin, not in your skull. That location is what your brain says your hand is.

✜ Thought →
The operator stays behind your eyes, not in your hand. That location is what your brain says your head is.

Same system, two tricks. You never feel the swap. The story just feels natural.

✜ If the anchor moved →
For most of us, the sense of "me" trained itself on eyes, ears, and balance. 

In blind or deafblind people, touch and body sense pick up more of the work. Body maps diverge from the sighted template. The felt anchor may shift from skull to torso or whole body. Nobody has measured that directly yet.

Press your elbow to the desk. Try to feel the next thought start in the joint. The skin reports pressure, not a speaker. The words still land behind your eyes.

The elbow says absolutely nothing.

Same machine. Two locations. One feels obvious.
