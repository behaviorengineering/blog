The Mirror That Only Blames

Ever notice someone who can explain every crisis except their part in it? Picture a vampire checking the mirror after a feeding. The glass is empty, so the mess must be the light, the furniture, or you.

That is the tell. "I caused this" never makes the menu. They scan faces for a culprit and the nearest person wins.

The rest of us learn the rules fast. Call out the damage and you become the next target. So we freeze, swallow the objection, and quietly clean up so they never have to see it.

Each time we stay quiet, we hand them another blank mirror. Silence reads like proof that nothing is wrong. The bill lands on bystanders, not on the person who cannot reflect.

You cannot fix a missing reflection by hiding the damage.
