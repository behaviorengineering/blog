"Fear Runs The Chain"

❓ Everyone tracks the mood above them, so truth gets filtered at every layer. The system says "report risk," but rewards "don't trigger fear."

AND:
This is a fear chain: each layer feels more danger from upsetting superiors than from the actual problem. Incentives like promotions, reviews, and blame exposure push people to soften bad news, delay escalation, or reroute it sideways.

Narrative control emerges inside the organisation: signals get translated into safer language before they move upward. This feels professional because it protects relationships and status. "Bad news shrinks as it climbs." Without filters for honesty, courage, and reality-contact, systems select people who manage perception instead of confronting risk early.
