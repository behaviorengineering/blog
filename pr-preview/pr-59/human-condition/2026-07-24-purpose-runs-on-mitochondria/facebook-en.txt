⚡ Why does purpose change your mitochondria?

Look: energy is real. Most of us feel it as that gap between having the agency to change our lives and barely being able to finish the day.

It turns out your body runs on a fixed energy budget. When you're stressed, ruminating, or feeling threatened, your body acts like it's in a crisis. It spends that budget on heart rate and tension, leaving repair and brain maintenance in the back of the queue.

The wild part? We carry about five quadrillion ancient bacteria (mitochondria) that handle this metabolic work for us. They're the ones turning food into the electricity that moves your thoughts.

When you have a sense of purpose, research shows your mitochondria actually work more efficiently. Purpose acts like a magnet, focusing your cellular "spend" on what you decide is worth it.

So instead of asking "How do I get more energy?", try asking "Where is my budget going?" and "What is worth the spend?"
