🚦 Developed countries score the machine, not your life

We still aspire to rich nations. HDI and World Happiness rankings mostly track how well the machine runs, not how you live, relate, or feel.

Call them well-serviced factories. High GDP, services that work, people kept busy enough to work and consume. Everyone watches everyone so no one steps out of line. Growth first. Power stays put.

✜ When the engine accelerates
No one talks about the time you lose for the people who matter. The factory cannot measure a thick relationship, so it will not protect one.

✜ Cushions do not move power
Some countries add health nets and checks and balances so the system does not blow up those living inside it. The real leap is when citizens keep the State in check. That is rare, and usually there is no energy left.

✜ Life at the center
Work that sustains your people, not just fuels the machine. Family over productivity. Money in service of life. Autonomy to step away without ending on the street.

The factory will not slow down for you. Build something else, or get pulled back in.
