What doesn't make you stronger, makes you weaker

The Bajau people spend 60% of their working life underwater. Their spleens evolved to be 50% larger, squeezing out oxygen-rich blood like a built-in scuba tank.

✜ Genes move fast under pressure. The Turkana adapted to survive on almost no water, and Andeans evolved to drink water with 20 times the safe arsenic level.

✜ But in rich countries, we don't get stronger from stress, we route around it. Culture and technology remove the pressure before our biology can catch up.

✜ We patch myopia with glasses and bypass biological limits with C-sections. We are becoming a superorganism where survival depends entirely on the group's infrastructure.

As long as the system holds, this weakness is invisible. But if the environment shifts hard and fast, we'll discover that what didn't have a chance to kill us, never had a chance to make us stronger.
