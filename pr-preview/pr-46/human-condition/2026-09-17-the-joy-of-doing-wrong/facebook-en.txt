Ever notice you sometimes do the wrong thing for no payoff at all?

St. Augustine stole pears he did not even want, then threw them to the pigs. No hunger. No profit. Just the itch to break a rule because it was a rule.

That itch shows up in tiny ways too. Finger in the ice cream. A pointless little rebellion that only means: I still choose.

And when someone tries to script you too hard, the same itch can get louder. During COVID, some people refused the shot because the mandate itself felt like a grab at their sovereignty. Reactance: push back to prove you are still unmanageable.

Banksy shredded a painting at auction and rewrote the game. Same family of moves, bigger stage.

Autonomy is human. Pure reactance is not a plan. You can end up defined by what you refuse, not by what you want.

So look at your last defiance. Was it aimed at something, or just a way to feel free in front of a rule?
