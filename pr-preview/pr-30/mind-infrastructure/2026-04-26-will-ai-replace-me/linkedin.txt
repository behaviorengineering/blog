🤖 Will AI replace you? The uncomfortable truth about work

The real risk is the colleague, vendor, or new hire who produces acceptable work faster and cheaper by treating models as leverage while others defend process for its own sake.

What matters is comparative advantage in a world where mechanical cognition is partially commoditized. Debating whether AI is "truly" thinking is a distraction from the immediate economic shift.

✜ The spell-check mirror
Spell-check shifted the value of writing: it removed the prestige of memorizing exceptions and pushed the creative job up a layer. Ideas, structure, taste, and persuasion now matter more than catching every doubled letter.

This pattern repeats wherever software absorbs complex workflows. Human value shifts to the messy parts: priorities, ethics, and context that does not fit a template.

✜ The industrial illusion
The industrial illusion is the belief that following rules and staying low-conflict guarantees stability. That bargain made sense when organizations needed predictable, interchangeable execution. It now fails as machines scale execution better than any human pipeline.

The uncomfortable truth: predictability is exactly what you automate first.

✜ The evolutionary test
Can you still operate in the band evolution optimized for, variation under uncertainty, or have you been trained into repeatable compliance?

Leverage the capabilities that are expensive for institutions to fake. Aim to be the anomaly they cannot average away, not the profile the pipeline already expects.
