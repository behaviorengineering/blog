💊🌀 Why might someone pass exams on LSD without notes?

At uni a housemate told me about a friend who showed up to one class on LSD every time. No notebook, no laptop, yet he supposedly passed every exam. The story sat in my head for years.

Now that psychedelic research is everywhere, I wondered if there is anything to it besides campus legend.

Speculative science only, not a recommendation. Classic psychedelics can open a brief plasticity window: the brain rewires more easily for hours or days after a dose.

A low-dose study checked people the next day and found better episodic memory and verbal fluency, but worse flexibility when the rules changed without warning. Recent inputs weigh too heavy, like ML overfitting: recall jumps, adapting to new rules sinks.

Same lecture on repeat, brain extra plastic, almost stuck on what is in front of you: maybe you stamp the material in with unusual strength. I never verified the story. Thought experiment, not advice.
