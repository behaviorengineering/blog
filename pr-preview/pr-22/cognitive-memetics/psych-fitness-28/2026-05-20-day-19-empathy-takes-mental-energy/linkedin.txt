✔️ THE FACTS:

August 1942.

Churchill visited troops in North Africa. He spoke with soldiers who had been in combat for months. He sat with them. He listened. His capacity for empathy remained intact despite the weight of the war.

Empathy requires mental energy. When leaders stretch beyond their limits, that capacity drops. Protecting mental fitness protects the ability to serve the public.

The connection is direct.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.
