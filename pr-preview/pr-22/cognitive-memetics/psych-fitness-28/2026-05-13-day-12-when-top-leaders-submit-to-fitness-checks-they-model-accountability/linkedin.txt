✔️ THE FACTS:

June 1944.

Eisenhower wrote a letter accepting full blame for D-Day failure before the invasion began. He knew the risks. He owned them publicly. He modeled accountability for the entire Allied command.

Accountability is not weakness. It is a maintenance requirement for high-stakes leadership. When top leaders submit to fitness checks, they demonstrate that power carries responsibility.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.
