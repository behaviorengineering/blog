February 1945.

Roosevelt crossed the Atlantic already exhausted for the Yalta Conference. Allies worried about his stamina. He still faced decisions on the Pacific war and the future United Nations.

We track economic performance with rigorous metrics. We apply clear standards to financial decisions. We need the same clarity for the readiness of the people making them.

Objective standards protect everyone.
