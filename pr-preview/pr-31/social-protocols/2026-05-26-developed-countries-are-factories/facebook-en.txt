🚦 Ever notice we still treat "developed countries" like the finish line?

HDI and World Happiness mostly score how well the machine runs: GDP, services, rankings. Not whether you have time for the people who matter.

Call them well-serviced factories. Healthy, educated, busy enough to work and consume. Everyone watches everyone so nobody steps out of line. Growth first. Power stays put.

Some countries soften the blow: health, pensions, safety nets. The Nordics even put checks in place so the system does not blow up those living inside it. The hard leap is when citizens keep the State in check. That is rare, and usually there is no energy left.

A real society puts life in the center: work that sustains your people, not just feeds the machine. Family over productivity. Money in service of life.

The factory will not slow down for you. Build something else, or get pulled back in.
