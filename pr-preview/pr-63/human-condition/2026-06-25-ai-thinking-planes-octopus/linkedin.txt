🦑🧠 You think you think?

People say AI is taking our thinking. Partly true, but mostly on the habit plane: inherited conclusions, reactive loops, cognition on muscle memory.

What stays yours is the metacognition plane: noticing what you outsourced, and learning to think ahead of your thoughts.

✜ Much of what we call thinking is following someone else's script. Opinions arrive pre-packaged. Reactions fire before you ask why.

✜ We are less like one unified mind and more like an octopus. Its arms carry dense neuron clusters; they explore and decide locally while the central brain sets goals.

✜ Your routine arms (scroll, react, let AI draft and sort) run on autopilot. Recommendation systems, LLMs, and planners become extra arms reaching into the world for you.

✜ Work shifts from moving pixels to choosing among many drafts at once. A designer who once spent nine hours nudging elements now evaluates a dozen proofs-of-concept at selection speed.

✜ Treat every arm as the whole mind and nothing inside feels awake.

✜ An arm moving is not a mind waking up. You can watch TV while your limbs run. Or you can watch your limbs like the octopus brain: track what you outsourced and what you abandoned.

Thinking ahead of your thoughts means directing the parallel spread. Set the target, let the artificial limbs map the territory, pick the pattern that hits the mark.
