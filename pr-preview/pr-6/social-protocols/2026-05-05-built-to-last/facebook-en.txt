🧵 Ever notice how the big promise almost never takes the blame?

Look: the systems that stick around rarely need to bully you. They make you want to play along, because belonging and purpose feel good. Your obedience starts to feel like participation.

Here is the old playbook. First they give you a goal you can chase forever but never finish. Then they wrap it in rules, noble stories, and little rituals so it feels real. Finally they put interpreters in charge: priests, managers, judges, the people who turn the ideal into who gets rewarded and who gets shamed.

They pay you in human currency: status, meaning, a seat at the table.

When something breaks, the trick is always the same. Bad leader. Corrupt priest. Users who "did not adopt it right." The mission, the constitution, the AGI dream stays pure; only the messenger is flawed.

Question the rules and the group often does the defending for you. Minimize you, shame you, freeze you out until you stop pushing or stop belonging.

New tech, same campfire. The chase never ends; the ideal never gets convicted.
