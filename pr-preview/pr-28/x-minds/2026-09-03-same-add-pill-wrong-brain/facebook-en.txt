🧠 Why can the same ADD medicine ruin one brain and save another?

Diagnosing ADD from a checklist alone is like asking a mechanic to fix an engine by listening to the sounds and never opening the hood. Psychiatrists who never look at the brain work the same way.

Same symptoms can come from opposite patterns. Classic ADD often looks underactive up front, so stimulants can help. Ring of Fire and overfocused patterns can be overactive. Stimulants pour fuel on that fire.

One case hit hard: a woman treated for "depression" with Prozac tipped into public disinhibition at a stoplight. Imaging, stopping the drug, and a targeted stimulant ended the crisis.

What matters is what is happening inside your head, and how you bring that pattern under control. Match the plan to the brain you have, not a one-size ADD pill.
