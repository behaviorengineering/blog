🧩 Ever notice how a simple date or a clear intent can land like a personal attack?

You try to clear things up, and the talk goes sideways into chaos. One of you uses facts to settle the picture; the other treats that clarity like a threat. Then the line shows up: "I feel attacked by the facts, so you must be attacking me."

That is not just a bad day. It is a different way of handling reality and blame. When their preferred story has to stay on top, an irrefutable fact feels like invasion. They bend reality around the feeling, drop what happened, and center the fight on their reaction. Rage often means fear of losing the narrative, not guilt over a detail.

You cannot fix a fight if you cannot agree what happened. If they protect their version at all costs, "communicate better" will not save it. Better to avoid contact and set limits than burn another heroic conversation. Any bond that asks you to drop checkable facts for "peace" is already broken.
