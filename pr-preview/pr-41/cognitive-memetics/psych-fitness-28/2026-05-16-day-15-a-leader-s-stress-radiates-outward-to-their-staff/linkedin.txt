✔️ THE FACTS:

January 1986.

Engineers warned that cold weather could compromise the shuttle's O-rings. NASA managers pushed to launch anyway. The pressure to stay on schedule radiated down the chain. Dissent was buried.

Challenger broke apart seventy-three seconds after liftoff. A stable leader creates space for good advice to surface. An unstable chain creates fear-based compliance. Psychological fitness at the top protects the entire decision chain.

➕ THE ASK:

We all run on the same human hardware. Let's build the support and checks to match the pressure of the job.
